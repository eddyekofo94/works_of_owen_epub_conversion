Κατοπτρίζω does not admit of the signification here ascribed to it by Dr Owen. Another view is taken of the passage, by which a tacit antithesis is instituted between κάτοπτρον and ἐικών: "Dominus nos κατοπτρίζει, splendorem faciei suae in corda nostram, tanquam in specula immittens: nos illum splendorem suscipimus et referimus.
